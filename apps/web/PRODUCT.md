# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BFB is for small technical teams coordinating several coding agents across many repositories. A typical workspace has three people, ten projects, five named agent profiles, and multiple Macs. Users arrive to answer the highest-value human question, see who or what owns each task, hand work to an allowed agent or teammate, and review committed work without reconstructing context from separate coding apps.

## Product Purpose

BFB is an attention-first work board and provider-neutral control plane. It shows where work is, what changed, why a human is needed, what can be delegated, and which facts are unavailable. Success means a person can understand the workspace and act on its three most urgent requests within seconds while agents use the same task and context contracts through APIs and MCP.

## Brand Personality

Direct, irreverent, operational. BFB is confident enough to be funny, but never jokes at the expense of clarity or truth. It speaks like a sharp teammate who hates ceremony and wants the work moving.

## Anti-references

- Jira-style configurable workflow machinery, status-column sprawl, health scores, and management dashboards.
- Generic SaaS dashboards made from interchangeable rounded cards, decorative metrics, gradients, glass, or soft shadows.
- Terminal cosplay that makes ordinary product actions cryptic.
- Interfaces that imply an agent is working, a human reviewed something, or time and tokens were measured when no authoritative record exists.
- Project colors used as side stripes or priority colors reused as project identity.

## Design Principles

1. Route attention before showing inventory: the few tasks that need the current human come first.
2. Make ownership and the next action unmistakable: say why the human is needed or name the allowed handoff.
3. Show committed facts and explicit unavailable states; never manufacture presence, completion, review, time, or token data.
4. Keep projects spatially stable and tasks compact: horizontal lanes are projects, never status columns.
5. Favor fast, familiar controls and dry humor over workflow configuration or decorative spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for the authenticated app. Every action must be keyboard operable with visible focus, semantic names, useful error text, and reduced-motion support. Project, priority, state, and ownership must remain distinguishable without color alone, and the board must remain usable with zoom, narrow viewports, and assistive technology.
