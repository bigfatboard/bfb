## Remaining boundaries

Mounted route and native proofs are separate. The native harness uses synthetic
retained OAuth metadata over disposable D1, not real OAuth/cookie/CLI ingress;
its actual Worker/DO RPC is not deployment, residency or live-provider proof.
Final selection followed by synchronous serialization is the proven boundary,
not authority at arbitrary later delivery time or rollback of valid committed
effects.

GitHub collision policy/internal helper bypass, execution-owned private delivery,
destructive retention, artifact-byte consumers and broader temporal/lease
boundaries remain open. No private activation, fresh visual, installed-app,
persistent-pilot, deployment or external-CI certificate is included. The other
mandatory product features remain separate unfinished work.
